\begin{problem}{H}{Harvest}{2 seconds}

\noindent
The annual Harvest Festival is approaching, and you control an autonomous Harvesting Drone that tends to a single row of $N$ fields, numbered from $1$ to $N$ from left to right. Field $i$ currently requires $v_i$ units of fuel to harvest; whenever the drone lands on a field, it must harvest it, consuming that many units of fuel.

The drone always moves strictly to the right using one of its three thrusters:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Short hop}: advance exactly $1$ field.
  \item \textbf{Medium hop}: advance exactly $2$ fields.
  \item \textbf{Long hop}: advance exactly $3$ fields.
\end{itemize}
To avoid overheating, the drone's control unit refuses to fire the same thruster twice in a row --- consecutive hops must use different lengths.

You must process $Q$ events of the following two types:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \texttt{1 l r}: Report the minimum total fuel the drone needs to travel from field $l$ to field $r$, obeying the movement rules above. The total includes the harvesting cost of field $l$, field $r$, and every field the drone lands on in between.
  \item \texttt{2 a b}: A replanting crew changes the harvesting cost of field $a$ to $b$.
\end{itemize}

\InputFile

\noindent
The first line contains two integers $N$ and $Q$.\\
The second line contains $N$ space-separated integers $v_1, v_2, \ldots, v_N$ --- the initial harvesting cost of each field.\\
Each of the next $Q$ lines contains one event in one of the two formats described above.

\OutputFile

\noindent
For each event of type \texttt{1}, print a single integer on its own line: the minimum total fuel required to travel from field $l$ to field $r$.

\section*{\sffamily{Constraints}}

\begin{itemize}
  \item $2 \le N, Q \le 10^5$
  \item $1 \le v_i, b \le 10^9$
  \item $1 \le l \le r \le N$
  \item $1 \le a \le N$
\end{itemize}

\Examples

\begin{example}
\exmp{1}{
6 3
3 1 4 1 5 9
1 1 6
2 4 10
1 1 6
}{13
16
}
\Explaination{Initially the drone can travel from field $1$ to field $6$ using a long hop then a medium hop (landing on fields $1, 4, 6$), harvesting $3+1+9=13$ fuel, which is optimal. After the update, field $4$'s cost becomes $10$, so the cheapest route switches to a medium hop then a long hop (landing on fields $1, 3, 6$), harvesting $3+4+9=16$ fuel.}
\end{example}

\end{problem}
